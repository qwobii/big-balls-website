BIG BALLS

A bowling alley stuck happily between two decades — chrome lanes, UV paint, and a jukebox that never left the 90s.

What's on the deck

Twelve lanes, a full bar, and an arcade wall that hums louder than the subwoofers.

Glow Lanes

Twelve blacklight lanes with house balls in six neon colorways. Bumpers available on request.

Backroom Arcade

DDR, Skee-Ball, and a claw machine nobody has ever won. Token machines up front.

The Pin Bar

Slushie cocktails, gutter fries, and a birthday-party package that includes the good disco ball.

League Nights

Tuesday and Thursday leagues, first frame free for new teams. Sign-up sheet's on the counter.

Private Parties

Buy out the back six lanes for birthdays, wraps, and questionable corporate team-building.

Pro Shop

Grips, tape, and a small wall of merch — the "Big Balls" tee outsells everything else we stock.
